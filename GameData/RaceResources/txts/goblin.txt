Tall, slender, and naturally grumpy, Goblins are the reclusive survivalists of the ogroid family. They possess a vast array of physical traits, with skin shifting from green to yellow or red, and head structures ranging from flat to elongated. However, that doesn't mean some aren't attractive by human standards. These Ogroids are notoriously isolationist and hard to befriend, and their keen senses make the crowded stone walls and artificial noise of cities extremely overwhelming, leading to urban disorientation. 

Having formed a profound bond with the natural world around them, Goblins can speak with and understand beasts, and possess a high natural resistance to lethal toxins. Consequently, they dwell in "Living Cities" made of bone and vines within the canopies of the Murd'Akhar jungles. While they begrudgingly joined the empire for safety, they tend to dislike the Drow’s manipulative methods and the Orcs’ brutal violence and only serve, when needed, as the empire’s best outriders and scouts.

Goblins that never left their jungles remain fiercely suspicious of outsiders. And while they can welcome other Goblins and Gremlins, whom they consider their closest kin, Drows and Ogres avoid the danger of going unguided into deep Goblin territory.
