Beaked, completely feathered and widely varied in shapes as in colors, the Asvyn have a beauty at odds with their origin, when they once darkened the skies of Khadash.
Nowadays largely nomadic, the Asvyn value freedom over having a permanent home, and can be seen all over the two continents after discovering their incredible ability to adapt easier than others to extreme deserts, frozen tundras, deep jungles and inhospitable mountain peaks.
Appearance holds great importance in Asvyn cultures, through groomed and decorated beaks and feathers, colorful fabrics and shining jewelry, which even the humblest of Asvyn wears.

- The [b]Aramakus[/b], meaning "free wings", have wings on their backs as well as free arms. They are considered the most noble and free of all Asvyn, and often display the most colorful plumage.

- The [b]Rauku[/b], "bound wings", whose arms extend into wings, are the fastest because of their arm-wings taking all their upper body muscles. They are the most common among this race.

- The [b]Kenkus[/b] have no wings. While this places them lower in Asvyn society, they are usually better physical fighters, and have a reputation for being highly resourceful in all nations.
