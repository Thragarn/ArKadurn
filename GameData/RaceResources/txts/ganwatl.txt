The Ganwatl, in some mistranslations written as Gunaawatl, are a race that's remarkably absent from history, whose tale of oblivion is heavily bound to that of Icoaxan (Ikoachan), their main home today. These forgotten hominids eventually returned to our world a century and a half ago, along with their cities and the beasts they call "dinosaura".
Ganwatl thrive in hot and humid climates, where dangers are as abundant as the vegetation that hides them. Their minds are more reptilian than their appearance, for conversations are only an exchange of information, and empathy demands conscious effort. In Icoaxan, a pet may become a meal as easily as a companion that has proven too useless.

- The [b]Az'atotl[/b] are the most common among these lizard-folk. Social yet devoid of empathy, they gaze with pity and wonder upon the other races, who somehow survived this long without claws or scales.

- The [b]Kekoatla[/b] are smooth-skinned amphibians. More capable of empathy, they adapted the fastest to life among other races, and some of their explorers have already reached lands as far as Feijen.

- The [b]Nagas[/b] are the rarest of the Ganwatl, shrouded in mystery even among their own kind. Extremely solitary, they are said to heal themselves by drinking poison and to use their understanding of others’ emotions to better trick them.
