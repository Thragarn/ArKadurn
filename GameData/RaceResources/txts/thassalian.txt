Thassalians are amphibious hominids that mainly inhabit the underwater cities of Duwilwen. While varied in shapes and colors, most Thassalians have deep, dark blue scales, a strong tail that serves as their main aquatic propeller, and a good-looking yet barely humanoid head.
But what characterizes Thassalians most is that some of their scales are not only capable of bioluminescence, but they can do so in any shade that hominid eyes can perceive. These scales are used for underwater communication and contribute to the charming appearance of these amphibians, but can also betray their stronger emotions.
Their bodies also secrete an oil that improves their hydrodynamics and, while not necessarily unpleasant, makes them rather easy to smell.
Overall, Thassalians are well-liked by other hominids for their tendency to have a humble nature and cordial manners, a gentleness usually credited to the absence of territorial and resource competition with surface races. This peaceful coexistence let time largely bury the memory of their darker past.
