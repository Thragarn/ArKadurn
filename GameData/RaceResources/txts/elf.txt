Similar to humans, elves are tall, elegant, and pointy-eared humanoids. Their taste for arts and all things refined, high attunement to magic, combined with the general beardlessness of their male population gave them a false reputation of physical weakness, though this is far from being the case.
Many envy the long lifespans of Elves, but few realize the curse that it is; aside from a passionate or disciplined few, the vast amount of time these humanoids possess takes away all sense of urgency to truly put in the effort into what they do and only in their last 50 years, when their youth finally starts to slip away, do they realize this truth. Some Elves feel a deep, quiet insecurity around humans whom they view as terrifyingly intense, burning twice as bright for half as long.
They are as diverse as humans, and can be divided into three subraces:

- [b]High Elves[/b] are the most common variant, descending from the noble families of the old civilisation. They are the most widespread of their kind, found primarily in Silmaran, but also in great numbers in Drascor, Feijen, Ashelha and Hirimgaard. Because of their subrace's name combined with their history, many individuals suffer an ever so slight, although mostly harmless, superiority complex.

- Recognizable by their silver hair, [b]Moon Elves[/b] are rare and widely viewed as a peaceful,  nature-loving people with a great penchant to healing professions. They are the least adventurous subrace, sticking to their ancestral homes hidden deep within the forest for the most part.

- Said to be descendants of the first dopplers, [b]Drow[/b] have violet skin that is said to help them sneak in the shadows, white or ink black hair, and eyes that shimmer in dim lighting. They suffer from a long-standing reputation of being perfidious, though much of this prejudice is due to ancient history and their failed coup in Silmaran. Following their expulsion from the fae kingdom two centuries ago, they moved north-eastwards to lay foundation to Murd'Akhar. Today, they are mainly found in northern Caeros: Hirimgaard, Drascor, and Murd'Akhar, where their greatest numbers reside.
