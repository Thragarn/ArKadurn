Impatient, highly competent and somewhat arrogant to say the least, Illithids make for atypical individuals that are hard to miss unless they wish to be. Their octopus-like heads are only part of it: blessed with great minds but cursed with short lives, they chase grandeur as if their lives depended on it, leading them to accomplish great deeds, usually in intellectual fields. However it is not rare that these ambitions get overtaken by impatience, turning many an Illithid into an accidental and unfortunate villain.
Illithids were created, during the ancient times, to help magic and scholarly research. It is said that some of them even participated in creating some of the latter zoomorphic races.
These humanoids are undeniably mildly averse to individuals they may perceive as more passive, viewing their kin as the most reliable associates. This encyclopedic opus, for instance, is the work of such an Illithid.
