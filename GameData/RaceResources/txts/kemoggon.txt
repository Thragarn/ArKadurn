Kemoggon designates zoomorphs resembling mammalian herbivores and cetaceans. Created during the age of the catastrophe, Kemoggons, then called Thaürs, mainly served as bodyguards and domain guards for the most important families of Uram. However, they were also the first to side with the people's revolution against the Heliomancer. In Ashelha as in Silmaran, they are still designated by that ancient name.
Kemoggons fall into recognisable branches, distinct enough to have their own subracial name though too many to count, and with little to set them apart in behavior or culture:
Centaurs resemble Equidae; Satyrs Caprinae; Minotaurs Bovidae; Baltaurs have fully adapted to marine or semi-marine life; and many other groups yet unmentioned exist.
To this day, Kemoggons carry behaviors and traditions reminiscent of their ancient role, the most notable being their acute senses and a high reactivity that makes them the worst nightmare of tavern owners.
