Among the very first experimental races created during the times of Uram, Iyawanas are almost as varied as their Kaphrax cousins. Both sleepy and hyperactive, the only common trait among these folk is their high agility, explosive energy and love for quick naps.
Iyawana families are diverse, but can be broadly classified into three subraces:

- The [b]Tunwe[/b] are the largest conglomerate of families, sharing and swapping a wide range of traits. They represent the stereotype of the Iyawana, amongst whom you'd find those resembling Muscada, Saltipeda, Xylovora, Fossorea and Clypeata[b]*[/b] amongst others.
Today, it is not uncommon to find them populating the undergrounds and wildernesses of the great towns and cities of the world, on both continents.

- The [b]Hunbwu[/b] are the most physically imposing amongst the Iyawana, resembling simians. They were the first to be experimented on and are thus the least modified zoomorphs, both physically and mentally. They mostly live on the borders of the Icoaxan jungle, where some tribes are known for their unusually violent methods.

- The [b]Nyctis[/b] are a special family of flying Iyawana, reminiscent of bats. Mostly nocturnal, hence the name, they were created to become spies during the times of Uram, and tend to fill that exact role when serving in foreign kingdoms far from their homeland in western Feijen.

[b]*[/b]: For our young and less versed readers, this would read as the families of rats & mice, hares & rabbits, beavers, moles and armadillos respectively, as of the current classification of the bestiarists and sages.
