This insectoid hominid race is among the most unique in Ar'Kadurn. Their traits vary like no other yet a few are universal, for beneath their compound eyes, mandibles for a mouth and  chitin-like skin still lies a mostly humanoid skeleton. Scholars believe this lineage endured the most experiments during the Urudshahag era, giving them their unparalleled diversity across all of the continent of Caeros, with some even inhabiting Uram's cursed lands.
While as capable of civility as other races, Kaphrax typically avoid populated areas because of the prejudice caused by their appearance and the fact that however hard they try, their mandibles give their speech an unsettling sonority.
Kaphrax are very pragmatic, and can see things disgusting to others as completely normal, or at least as harmless. For example, anything grilled is considered a delicacy, as long as it was animal beforehand.
