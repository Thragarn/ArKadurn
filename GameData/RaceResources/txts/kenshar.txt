Created for absolute loyalty, Kenshars were a race engineered as Uram's most terrible weapon against its own people. And when their creator fell, so did the weight of their own actions.
Driven by guilt, the Kenshars exiled themselves to the steppes beneath the Vhaatur mountains, and swore an oath to never serve a king again. This oath still shapes their society today as they formed the Kahans, a nomadic people that obey no authority greater than one's own clan.
Built for war rather than craft, Kenshar anatomy makes them poor artisans that rely on trade with neighboring nations and visiting caravans. Furthermore, their often intimidating appearance often hides deeply loyal and affectionate folk.

- [b]Ulfengars[/b] are Kenshars resembling canids. The most numerous, they are highly social and loyal, yet from amongst them came the cruel [b]Gnolls[/b], who live in matriarchal communities near the cursed lands, and the [b]Kitsune[/b], who exiled themselves to found the Shogunate after rejecting the oath.

- [b]Krohulgars[/b], who resemble ursids, are gentle giants that can turn incredibly ferocious when provoked. They mainly live along with Ulfengars, although some migrated to Hirimgaard for its life of adventure and its better suited climate.

- [b]Kathars[/b] owe their exceptional agility to their feline traits. Solitary and pragmatic, many of them traveled away from the steppes, especially to Feijen.

- Some [b]Ötgars[/b] resemble mustelids, others aquatic carnivores. Drawn at first to the promise of a new Kenshar nation, many fled the Shogunate once it turned into a Kitsune supremacy, settling in Feijen and Ashelha.
