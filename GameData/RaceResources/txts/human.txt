The most polyvalent, adaptable and numerous of all the races of the continent, humans claim to be the closest descendants of the ancient Hominids.
History remembers them as a paradox, as the greatest heroes who saved our world along with the most cruel villains who sought to destroy it were mostly humans.
Humans are diverse in appearance, but they truly differ little. Nonetheless, they can be categorized into three major cultures:

- The [b]Caeorans[/b], or [b]Caeorn[/b], are the most numerous humans. Native to the supercontinent Caeros, their history is a chronicle of endless wars, shaping cultures that value chivalry, discipline, and ambition.

- The [b]Yugin[/b] are the descendants of hominids who never lived in Uram. They dwell in their ancestral Feijen lands in southernmost Caeros, well past the steppes of the Kahans. Valuing freedom, hard work, and the wisdom of the journeyman, their secular communities operate independently, uniting only in the face of existential threats. This culture of self-reliance has shaped them into peerless merchants and master craftsmen.

- The [b]Nagani[/b], known to traders as [b]the people of Nag[/b], designate themselves as the [b]Aatun[/b] meaning "those who arrive". They are the descendants of the hominids that followed the prophet Hashuram on his exodus to the new continent of Nagaradh. Nagani cultures are scholarly and deeply spiritual, flourishing in a relative peace far from the never-ending conflicts of Caeorn.
